Learning Aims

Three Aims · A → C

The unit progresses from underlying fluid behaviour to lift and drag, then to the full mechanics of aircraft flight.

AIM A

Properties of fluids & fluid flow

Air density, viscosity, pressure gradients, the continuity equation and Bernoulli's theorem. Underpins everything that follows.

AIM B

Lift & drag on aerodynamic shapes

How airflow generates lift and drag on aerofoils and bluff bodies — coefficients, stall, boundary layers and scaling.

AIM C

Stability, control & mechanics of flight

Aircraft stability, primary and secondary control surfaces, manoeuvre loads and modern fly-by-wire envelope protection.

12-Week Delivery Schedule

~5 GLH per week

Each row links directly to the wind tunnel worksheet and tutorial videos where they apply.

Wk Topic Focus Key Theoretical Content Practical / Lab Activities Assessment Milestone
01 Fluid Properties & Atmospheric Physics Air density, viscosity, pressure gradients. The International Standard Atmosphere (ISA) model. Units, fluid statics. Calculating pressure changes with altitude. ISA atmosphere modelling spreadsheet. Density measurement of air at lab conditions. Diagnostic Quiz
02 Fluid Dynamics & Continuity Incompressible flow. Continuity A₁V₁ = A₂V₂. Mass flow rate conservation. Streamlines and control volumes. Venturi tube tracking and flow velocity calculations. Kit familiarisation: ▶ Assembly ▶ Set up Formative Problem Set
03 Bernoulli's Theorem Energy conservation in fluid flow. Static, dynamic and total pressure. P + ½ρV² = constant. Limits and assumptions. WS1 · Pitot-static tube ▶ Manual & electronic readings Lab Report Draft
04 Airfoil Geometry & Boundary Layers Chord, camber, thickness, angle of attack α. Laminar vs turbulent boundary layer transition. Flow separation. WS6 · Smoke visualisation ▶ Smoke visualisation ▶ Attaching experiments Assignment 1 Issued
05 Lift Generation Mechanics Pressure distribution across upper / lower aerofoil surfaces. Circulation. Lift equation L = ½ρV²·S·CL. Stall. WS3 · Aerofoil pressure mapping WS4 · Lift & drag ▶ Wind tunnel software Airfoil Analysis Peer Review
06 Drag Components & Minimisation Induced drag, parasite drag (skin friction, form, interference). Total drag curve. Lift-to-drag ratio. WS5 · Drag coefficient on standard shapes Assignment 1 Submission
07 Wind Tunnel Testing & Scaling Experimental aerodynamic methods. Reynolds number Re and Mach number M scaling laws. Tunnel blockage and corrections. WS2 · Cylinder flow patterns ▶ Available experiments Wind Tunnel Data Log Check
08 Aircraft Stability Mechanics Longitudinal, lateral and directional stability. Static vs dynamic stability (phugoid, dutch roll, spiral). Adjusting centre of gravity (CoG) on model gliders to map stable glide paths. Optional WS6 re-run with stable / unstable shapes. Assignment 2 Issued
09 Primary Flight Control Surfaces Aerodynamic action of ailerons, elevators and rudders. Pitch, roll and yaw axes. Hinge moments and control feel. Flight simulator session OR physical linkage rig inspection for control tracking and movement ratios. Control Surface Mapping
10 Secondary Controls & High-Lift Devices Flaps, slats, slots and spoilers. Effect on the lift / drag polar. Take-off and landing configuration management. WS7 · Independent investigation (flap study) Assignment 2 Submission
11 Manoeuvre Aerodynamics & Loads Forces in steady turns, climbs and descents. Load factors (g-loading) and structural limits. Constructing V-n (velocity-load factor) manoeuvre diagrams for a chosen aircraft. Assignment 3 Issued
12 Modern Actuation & Flight Envelopes Fly-by-wire (FBW), artificial stability augmentation (SAS) and flight envelope protection. Sidestick vs yoke. Analysing block diagrams of active control loops and automated trim systems. Airbus vs Boeing envelope philosophies. Assignment 3 · Final Review

Assessment Structure

3 assignments · aligned to A / B / C

Three summative assignments aligned to the three Learning Aims, scaffolded by weekly formative milestones and a final review.

Assignment Learning Aim Issued · Due Deliverable
A1 Fluid Fundamentals Issued Wk 4 · Due Wk 6 Lab report on pitot-static + smoke visualisation, with Bernoulli analysis and boundary-layer commentary.
A2 Lift, Drag & Testing Issued Wk 8 · Due Wk 10 Comparative aerofoil and drag-coefficient report; lift / drag polars across three aerofoils plus Reynolds-number commentary.
A3 Stability & Performance Issued Wk 11 · Due Wk 12 Stability case study with V-n diagram for a chosen aircraft and a written analysis of an FBW envelope-protection scheme.
