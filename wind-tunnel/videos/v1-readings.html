Manual & Electronic Readings

How to take pressure measurements with the U-tube manometer and with the built-in digital pressure sensor — the two reading methods learners use in Worksheet 1.

Key takeaways

01 · U-tube manometer

Water sits near the 50 mm mark. You record the difference in column heights — polarity doesn't matter, just magnitude.

02 · Digital pressure sensor

Always zero with the fan OFF. Ignore the minus sign in readings — work with magnitude in your calculations.

03 · Trade-offs

The manometer is visual and intuitive but slow to read precisely. The digital sensor is fast and high-resolution but needs careful zeroing.

04 · Cross-check

The two methods should agree within experimental error. Disagreement at low fan speeds usually points to manometer reading error rather than sensor drift.

Where this fits

Use this video in Week 3 (Bernoulli's Theorem) alongside Worksheet 1 — Pitot-static tube. Watching it before the lab cuts about 15 minutes off the set-up phase and reduces zeroing mistakes.
