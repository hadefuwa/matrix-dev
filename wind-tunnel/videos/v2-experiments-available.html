Key takeaways

What learners should leave with
01 · Four families of experiment

Pressure measurement (WS1, WS2, WS3), force measurement (WS4, WS5), flow visualisation (WS6) and open-ended design (WS7).

02 · Reusable kit

The same two-force module supports aerofoil testing (WS4) and bluff-body drag (WS5). The pressure ports work for cylinder (WS2) and aerofoil (WS3).

03 · Beyond the worksheets

Learners can 3D-print their own shapes (cars, hulls, turbines, helmets) and slot them in via the threaded shaft, then re-use any of the seven procedures.

04 · Reynolds-number reach

From 10% fan to 100% covers a wide range of Reynolds numbers — enough to study scaling effects and the drag crisis on spheres.

Where this fits

Week 7

Use this video in Week 7 (Wind Tunnel Testing & Scaling). By this point learners have done Worksheets 1, 3, 4 and 5 — this video gives them the broader context and sets up the open-ended Worksheet 7 due in Week 10.
