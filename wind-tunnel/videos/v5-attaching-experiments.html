How Experiments Are Attached

Fitting the experiment holders into the test section — pitot-static tube, tapped cylinder, aerofoil pressure rake and the two-force module — so they sit straight, true and securely.

Key takeaways

01 · Two thumb-nuts

Each holder seats on two stainless studs and is secured with two thumb-nuts. Finger-tight only — no tools needed.

02 · Orientation matters

Pitot tubes point INTO the flow. Cylinder protractor 90°-mark lines up with the vertical window marker. Aerofoil chord lines up parallel to the flow at α = 0°.

03 · Tubing and cables

Pressure tubing pushes onto the controller's ports with a firm twist. Two-force module cables only fit one way.

04 · Re-zero on every change

Every time you swap a holder, re-zero the relevant sensors with the fan off before you start a new reading session.

Where this fits

Use this video in Week 4 (Airfoil Geometry & Boundary Layers) the first time learners install a holder themselves — typically the smoke-visualisation test article in WS6, then the cylinder in WS2 the following week.
