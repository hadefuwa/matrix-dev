Wind Tunnel 125 — Assembly

First-build walkthrough of the Matrix Wind Tunnel 125 — what's in the box, how the parts fit together, and what to check before learners use it.

Key takeaways

01 · Major components

Inlet section, test section (125 × 125 mm), diffuser, fan housing and touch-screen controller. Each plugs into the next with positive locating features.

02 · Cables

Power, sensor and load-cell cables are colour-coded. They only go in one way round.

03 · Bench placement

Stable, flat surface with at least 300 mm clear behind the diffuser (so the exhaust air can escape without recirculating).

04 · First-power-on

Always perform with no holder fitted in the test section. Confirm fan ramps cleanly to 10%, 50% and 100%.

Where this fits

Use this video in Week 2 (Fluid Dynamics & Continuity) for kit familiarisation, before any experiments begin. Pair it with "How to set up the wind tunnel" so learners see assembly and first power-on back-to-back.
